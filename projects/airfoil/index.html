NACA 2412 vs. NACA 0012

Home/Projects/NACA 2412 vs. NACA 0012

Academic project

The project

I used MATLAB to compare the aerodynamic coefficients of a cambered NACA 2412 profile and a symmetric NACA 0012 profile.

The challenge

The aim was to examine how the two profiles behaved as the angle of attack changed.

My contribution

The work behind it

  1. 01

    I developed numerical work in MATLAB to evaluate lift and drag coefficients.

  2. 02

    I compared the coefficients across angles of attack.

  3. 03

    I plotted the results to examine the differences between the two profiles.

Outcome

What came out of it

An academic numerical comparison of two airfoils. The results are model-based, without experimental validation.

Methods & tools

  • MATLAB
  • Numerical modeling
  • Lift & drag coefficients
