Home/Projects/Tube Bending Machine Design

Academic project

Project overview

This project covered a tube-bending machine from its required functions to the mechanism and supporting frame.

I needed to connect the bending motion with an architecture that could be sized and considered for manufacture.

My contribution

What I did

I carried out functional analysis and developed the machine architecture. I worked through the mechanism kinematics and the structural sizing of the frame. I considered actuation, part manufacture and assembly as the design developed.

A closer look

Inside the work

Result

The outcome

A complete machine-design study covering the three-roll mechanism, component sizing, supporting frame and CAD assembly.

Methods & tools

  • Functional analysis
