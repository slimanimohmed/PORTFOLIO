Tube Bending Machine Design

Home/Projects/Tube Bending Machine Design

Academic project

The project

This project covered a tube-bending machine from its required functions to the mechanism and supporting frame.

The challenge

I needed to connect the bending motion with an architecture that could be sized and considered for manufacture.

My contribution

The work behind it

  1. 01

    I carried out functional analysis and developed the machine architecture.

  2. 02

    I worked through the mechanism kinematics and the structural sizing of the frame.

  3. 03

    I considered actuation, part manufacture and assembly as the design developed.

Outcome

What came out of it

A machine-design study covering the mechanism, frame and manufacturing considerations. It is presented as design work; physical machine performance has not been established.

Methods & tools

  • Functional analysis
  • Kinematic synthesis
  • Structural sizing
  • DFM
