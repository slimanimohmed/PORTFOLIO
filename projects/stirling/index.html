Home/Projects/Gamma Stirling Engine Thermal Analysis

Academic project

The project

I modeled a Gamma Stirling engine and studied its theoretical thermal cycle.

The challenge

The project connected the engine geometry with the pressure-volume cycle used to describe heat-to-work conversion.

My contribution

The work behind it

  1. 01

    I developed a parametric CAD model of the engine.

  2. 02

    I carried out theoretical P–V-cycle calculations.

  3. 03

    I evaluated thermal-to-mechanical conversion through the cycle analysis.

Outcome

What came out of it

An engine CAD study and theoretical thermal analysis. The work does not claim measured power or efficiency.

Methods & tools

  • Parametric CAD
  • Theoretical P–V cycle
  • Thermal calculations
