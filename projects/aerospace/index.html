Academic project

Project overview

I led a team of six students on a fuselage-section design project for the EMAN Aerospace competition.

We needed to explore weight reduction while understanding how changes to the structure affected its mechanical behavior.

My contribution

What I did

I coordinated the team’s design and calculation work. We built parametric CAD and studied the fuselage section through structural calculations and static and modal FEA. We iterated the geometry to explore the trade-offs between mass and structural behavior.

A closer look

Inside the work

Result

The outcome

Our team placed second among the external participating teams. The project gave us a design study linking CAD decisions to structural analysis.

Methods & tools

  • Parametric CAD
  • Structural calculations
  • Static & modal FEA
