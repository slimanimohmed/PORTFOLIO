Project overview

At Guide Valve, I designed industrial valve assemblies and prepared the drawings and documentation needed to communicate them.

Large assemblies need consistent models, dimensions and parts lists. Different valve sizes also create repeated configuration work.

My contribution

What I did

I modeled industrial valves in CATIA V5 and SolidWorks, including assemblies with more than 150 components. I prepared manufacturing drawings, dimensional and functional tolerances, GD&T and bills of materials. I linked parametric models to Excel and used VBA to generate size-specific configurations and export STEP files and drawings.

Result

The outcome

Parametric valve assemblies, configuration tools and manufacturing documentation.
