Industrial experience

The project

At Guide Valve, I design industrial valve assemblies and prepare the drawings and documentation needed to communicate them.

The challenge

Large assemblies need consistent models, dimensions and parts lists. Different valve sizes also create repeated configuration work.

My contribution

The work behind it

  1. 01

    I model industrial valves in CATIA V5 and SolidWorks, including assemblies with more than 150 components.

  2. 02

    I prepare manufacturing drawings, dimensional and functional tolerances, GD&T and bills of materials.

  3. 03

    I link parametric models to Excel and use VBA to generate size-specific configurations and export STEP files and drawings.

Outcome

What came out of it

Parametric valve assemblies, configuration tools and manufacturing documentation.
