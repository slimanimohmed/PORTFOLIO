Industrial experience

The project

At Flyson, I followed agricultural spraying work from the customer order to the last drone flight, including time in the field.

The challenge

I looked for time and energy losses in the workflow, with particular attention to mission preparation.

My contribution

The work behind it

  1. 01

    I observed spraying missions and reported where preparation and operating stages lost time or energy.

  2. 02

    I helped standardize stages with checklists, prepared a maintenance plan and took part in daily drone maintenance.

  3. 03

    I built an Odoo database for flight and customer tracking and worked on Python tools for flight and battery scheduling or analysis.

Outcome

What came out of it

The internship recorded a 60% reduction in mission-preparation time. The figure concerns preparation, rather than total flight time or energy use.

Customer information, flight records and operational maps remain private.

Methods & tools

  • Workflow observation
  • Checklists
  • Maintenance
  • Odoo
  • Python
