Academic project

The project

This academic project started with cranial scan geometry and continued through an implant concept and a two-part mold.

The challenge

The design had to follow irregular anatomical surfaces while providing clear implant boundaries and fixation interfaces.

My contribution

The work behind it

  1. 01

    I reconstructed the cranial geometry from 3D scan data and worked on the mesh and surfaces.

  2. 02

    I modeled the implant boundaries and fixation interfaces.

  3. 03

    I designed a two-part mold for prototype manufacture, with prototyping and mechanical evaluation included in the project work.

A closer look

Inside the work

Outcome

What came out of it

An academic implant concept and split-mold design connecting scan reconstruction, surface modeling and prototype manufacture.
