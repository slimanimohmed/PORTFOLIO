My profile

Engineering from design to production.

I’m Mohamed Slimani, a final-year Mechanical Engineering student at Université Euromed de Fès. My experience combines industrial product design, mechanical calculations, finite element analysis and hands-on manufacturing.

At Guide Valve I designed industrial valve assemblies of more than 150 components, supported by manufacturing drawings, GD&T, bills of materials and CAD automation. At TE Connectivity, I worked on automotive connector locking concepts using functional analysis, parametric CAD and ANSYS Mechanical FEA. At the UEMF manufacturing lab, I connected CAD/CAM to CNC machining and additive prototyping.

I also supported process improvement at Flyson, where internship records document a 60% reduction in mission-preparation time.
